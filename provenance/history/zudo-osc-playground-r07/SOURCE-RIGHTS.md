# Source files and rights

The R06 source-assets manifest is preserved under `reference/`. All 21 retained
supplier/creator files are reused byte-identically. Their original URLs and SHA-256
hashes appear in `parts/assets-manifest.json`. They are not claimed as new downloads.

ALPS: original STEP files, drawings, GIFs, PDFs, and Read-me-first.rtf notices are
retained. The old RK09L pot model is HISTORICAL ONLY in R07. The RS20H fader is the
active geometry reference, not proof of current supply or complete assembly fit.

Bourns: manufacturer PTV09 family PDF is retained. No exact Bourns STEP was recovered.
The 12x12 mm reservation is our planning geometry and must never be presented as
manufacturer CAD or a complete solder-pad courtyard.

PJ398SM: creator model from the AudioJacks library, with MIT license and original
metadata retained beside it. It is a family reference, not exact WQP518MA maker CAD.
The associated Thonk drawing does not establish EARS dress-nut compatibility.

New panel SVGs, scripts and prose may be edited for this project. Their availability
does not grant a new blanket redistribution license to retained vendor files.
Review terms before publishing vendor binaries in a public documentation site;
link to the original source instead when needed. No font binaries are included.
