# Source files and publication boundary

The generated panel artwork, local prototype code and design notes in this packet are project deliverables. Source references and third-party files under `parts/` retain their original ownership. Preserve source URLs, applicability caveats, hashes, and any supplied license files (including `parts/qingpu-wqp518ma/AudioJacks-LICENSE`).

Retained manufacturer datasheets and CAD are local engineering reference material. Their inclusion does not assert permission to republish them on a public website. Before deployment, review each source's terms; link to an original manufacturer document instead of hosting its bytes when rights are uncertain. Do not remove the distinction between exact-part, shared-family, generic, historical and nominal-envelope assets.

New R15 component decision records contain source URLs and paraphrased findings, not newly downloaded exact CAD. The previous ALPS rotary/fader files remain historical references and are not active R15 fader selections. There are no active faders.

NOISE2 is a programmed third-party product; no creator firmware or blank-chip equivalence is claimed or redistributed here. Order its programmed identity or obtain appropriate programming rights and factory service explicitly.

No font binaries are included. Locally installed fonts may be used for regeneration.
