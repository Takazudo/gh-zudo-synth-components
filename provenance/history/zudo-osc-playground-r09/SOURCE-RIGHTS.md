# Sources and rights

The component manufacturer PDFs, drawings, photos and STEP files retained under `parts/` remain the property of their original owners and retain their original notices. This package supplies them for this user's engineering review, not as a new license to publicly redistribute vendor assets. Consult the retained manufacturer read-me files and source sites before publishing.

The PJ398SM-family STEP is a community model, not an exact incoming-jack certification; its license and source record are alongside it. The ALPS fader CAD is explicitly historical and must not be used to misrepresent the new LED-fader candidate.

The panel primitives and R07 source are inherited from the user's prior conversation project. The new SVG icons and local dock artwork were inherited from R08; R09 modifies placement/spacing only. No generated poster, font binary, external browser script, or manufacturer logo is embedded.

`parts/research-sources.json` records the current source URLs, applicability and retention gaps. `parts/assets-manifest.json` preserves the inherited binary hashes and describes their R08 and R09 uses. Generated files have their own SHA256SUMS; a hash demonstrates byte identity, not technical validity.
