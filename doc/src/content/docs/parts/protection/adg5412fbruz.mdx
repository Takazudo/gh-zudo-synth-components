## Why it is useful

ADG5412FBRUZ is a candidate for protecting signal inputs against out-of-range or powered-off external voltages. Its fault protection is directional and conditional on the device’s specified terminals and supply states.

## Design cautions

Input protection does not automatically protect output drivers, feedback sense wires or reference receivers. Keep source/drain assignment, fault-current paths, enable defaults and missing-rail behavior explicit. The source instrument’s complete protection architecture remains open.
