## Why it is useful

A separate red emitter can distinguish overload from ordinary signal magnitude. That semantic distinction belongs to the comparator/driver circuit and the panel labeling, not to the LED alone.

## Design cautions

An output-level detector can miss clipping upstream of an attenuating VCA. Define which node and headroom the comparator observes. Package polarity, hot-current limit, light output and optical separation need their own checks.
