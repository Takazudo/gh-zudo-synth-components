## Why it is useful

A common 0603 LED was the early inexpensive indicator proposal. It remains useful when the front-panel spacing allows a larger package than the later 0402 selection.

## Design cautions

Color identity, brightness bin and hot current need exact evidence. White is not automatically warm white. A circle in the panel drawing does not establish an optical aperture or lightpipe, and the package must clear the adjacent jack/pot.
