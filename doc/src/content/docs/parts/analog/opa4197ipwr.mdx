## Why it is useful

OPA4197IPWR was considered for precision outputs, buffered multiples and high-impedance sample/hold or slew stages. Its source record distinguishes room-temperature bias/offset data from full-temperature limits.

## Design cautions

Low typical bias current is not a guarantee at hot ambient. Post-resistor feedback can improve DC accuracy but change the capacitive-load loop; cable/load stability must be modeled and measured with one fixed compensation network. Count all four amplifiers and both rails correctly.
