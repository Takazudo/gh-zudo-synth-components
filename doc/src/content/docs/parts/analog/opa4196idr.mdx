## Why it is useful

OPA4196IDR was selected in the source research for general audio, CV and indicator amplifiers, where multiplying quiescent current by a large number of amplifier sections becomes important.

## Design cautions

Keep per-amplifier and per-package current separate. Add load current, indicator current and temperature maxima to the rail budget. Precision, bandwidth, capacitive-load stability and output swing still need application-level checks; a rail-to-rail label is not an all-load guarantee.
