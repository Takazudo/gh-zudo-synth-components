## Why it is useful

LM13700M/NOPB contains two operational transconductance amplifier sections, linearizing diodes and buffers. It supports several synth functions when surrounded by the appropriate scaling and conversion circuits.

## Design cautions

An OTA produces a current-related output; it is not a drop-in voltage op amp or ready-made VCA module. Respect the small differential input range, bias-current programming, diode operation and output loading. Filter resonance and VCA gain are circuit properties.
