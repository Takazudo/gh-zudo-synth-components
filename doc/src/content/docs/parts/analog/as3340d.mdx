## Why it is useful

AS3340D is the ALFA SOIC-16 oscillator-core candidate. A complete voice still needs reference/scaling, timing parts, waveform conditioning, modulation, sync and calibration.

## Design cautions

Do not assume every 3340-branded device or suffix has interchangeable limits. Follow the exact negative-supply and current-limiting guidance. The source’s triangle/saw/pulse core does not by itself establish a sine output or calibrated LFO/audio range.
