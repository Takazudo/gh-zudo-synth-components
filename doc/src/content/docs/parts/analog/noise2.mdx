## Why it is useful

Electric Druid NOISE2 is a programmed noise-generator device, not a blank microcontroller. It offered a repeatable white/pink source before separate blue/brown shaping stages.

## Design cautions

Preserve the programmed identity and delivery method in procurement. This is digital pseudorandom generation, not a purely analog noise junction. Output spectra, filtering, levels, bandwidth and buffering still require measurement.
