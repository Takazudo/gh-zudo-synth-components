## Why it is useful

The 7-position top-entry GH header can carry a terminated harness to an interface PCB. This member is kept separate from its cable housing and contact even though all belong to one mating system.

## Design cautions

Use the manufacturer’s mounting-view numbering, mating height and wire/contact conditions. Pitch and header height are not a rigid board-stack dimension. Budget return currents and partial mating; supports and strain relief, not solder joints, carry operating forces.
