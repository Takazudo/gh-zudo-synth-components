## Why it is useful

SSHL-002T-P0.2 is the contact used with the GH housing system. It needs a compatible conductor/insulation range and qualified crimp process; a bag of contacts is not a no-soldering harness.

## Design cautions

Order completed, inspected harnesses or specify factory termination. Contact resistance, crimp resistance and conductor resistance all contribute to voltage drop and heating. Do not assume parallel ground wires share current equally.
