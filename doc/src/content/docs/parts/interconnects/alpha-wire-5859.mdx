## Why it is useful

This conductor was retained for the heavier board-to-board power/return proposal after the complete rail budget grew beyond the early preview allowance.

## Design cautions

The termination, supported bend, hot resistance and actual routed current path must be verified together. A large wire cannot repair a narrow PCB return neck, and no soldering should remain after delivery.
