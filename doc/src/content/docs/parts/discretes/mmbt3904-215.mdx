## Why it is useful

MMBT3904,215 is a surface-mount general-purpose NPN candidate for indicator sinks and control circuitry. It is not automatically a characterized analog-noise source.

## Design cautions

Check pin ordering, collector dissipation, saturation/base drive and reverse junction limits. Device gain variation and temperature prevent assuming a current sink is accurate solely because the transistor is familiar.
