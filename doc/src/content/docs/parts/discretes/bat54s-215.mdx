## Why it is useful

BAT54S,215 provides a specific dual-diode topology that can be useful in clamp or steering circuits. The suffix S is part of the electrical configuration, not just packaging.

## Design cautions

Verify the internal series connection and pinout before substituting another BAT54 variant. Rail clamps can inject current into an unpowered rail; they need a bounded fault-current and power-off path.
