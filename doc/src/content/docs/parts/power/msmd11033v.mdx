## Why it is useful

The inherited supply construction used this resettable-protection candidate. It remains useful as a case study in temperature-dependent current and voltage-drop limits.

## Design cautions

Hold current is not a temperature-independent load budget, and a PTC is not an ideal current limiter. Source, wire, trace and component survival during the trip interval must be analyzed. Do not reuse an earlier low-current supply protection part blindly in the full instrument.
