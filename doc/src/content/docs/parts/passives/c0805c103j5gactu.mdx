## Why it is useful

C0805C103J5GACTU is the initial 10 nF C0G hold-capacitor candidate. It is deliberately distinct from the much larger downstream slew capacitor.

## Design cautions

Leakage, dielectric absorption, contamination and acquisition time determine actual storage performance. Increasing capacitance reduces ideal droop from a given current, but does not eliminate offset, hold step or temperature effects.
