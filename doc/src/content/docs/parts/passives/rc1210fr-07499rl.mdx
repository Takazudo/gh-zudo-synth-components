## Why it is useful

RC1210FR-07499RL is a 499 Ω larger-package resistor candidate. Series chains were investigated to distribute opposite-drive fault voltage and power.

## Design cautions

Evaluate simultaneous fault current, each resistor’s working voltage and hot power rating, not just total resistance. A series resistor limits current but does not by itself establish safe backfeeding or unpowered amplifier behavior.
