## Why it is useful

RC0603FR-07100KL is a concrete 100 kΩ resistor identity, not a wildcard for every resistor in a synth. It provides a baseline for ordinary bias/divider or signal-stage parts.

## Design cautions

Use exact values and tolerances per function. Check working voltage, power derating and pulse exposure separately; a physically small resistor near a protected jack can still dissipate excessive fault power.
