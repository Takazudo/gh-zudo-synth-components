## Why it is useful

C0603C101J5GACTU is a 100 pF C0G candidate for small-signal timing, filtering or compensation. Its exact value and placement are circuit-dependent.

## Design cautions

Do not move a compensation capacitor through a connector because it looks electrically small. Parasitic capacitance and trace inductance can dominate, and changing it to make a model pass requires a documented stability analysis.
