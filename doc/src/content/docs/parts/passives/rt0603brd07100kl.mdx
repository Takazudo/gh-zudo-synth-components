## Why it is useful

RT0603BRD07100KL is a thin-film 100 kΩ option useful when resistor error and drift deserve separate treatment from general resistors.

## Design cautions

Initial tolerance, temperature coefficient, ratio tracking and long-term drift are different properties. Two discrete precision resistors do not automatically track like a specified matched network.
