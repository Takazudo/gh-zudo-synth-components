## Why it is useful

SN74HC14DR provides Schmitt-trigger logic after a signal has been made safe for its supply domain. It is useful for shaping already-conditioned gates and for defined state-machine inputs.

## Design cautions

It must not receive a bipolar modular signal directly. Check injection during power-off, thresholds at the actual supply, unused-input termination and local decoupling. Multiple gates share one physical supply pair.
