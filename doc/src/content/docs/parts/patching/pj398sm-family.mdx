## Why it is useful

The retained AudioJacks STEP is a useful three-dimensional reference for a common synth-jack shape. Its original author and MIT notice are included.

## Design cautions

It is community family geometry, marked untested in its source metadata. It must not be relabeled exact QingPu WQP518MA manufacturer CAD, and it proves neither terminal mapping nor dress-nut fit.
