## Why it is useful

The user chose black nuts for inputs and red nuts for outputs. These are mechanical parts of the jack assembly and a stable visual code, not merely a paint choice in the renderer.

## Design cautions

The earlier 8.3 mm outside-diameter drawing was provisional. Confirm actual thread, nut thickness, engagement, seating and jack-lot compatibility. Leave room for the tightening tool and the cable plug barrel.
