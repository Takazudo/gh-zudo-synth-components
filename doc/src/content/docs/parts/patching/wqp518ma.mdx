## Why it is useful

The WQP518MA/Thonkiconn research is useful because patch jacks define the panel’s mounting datum. The body stays behind the panel; the bushing passes through a normal mounting hole and is clamped by the selected nut.

## Design cautions

QingPu names, PJ398SM family drawings and reseller codes are not a proven exact-variant equivalence. Match sleeve/tip/switch contacts, shoulder height, thread and nut engagement to the actual lot. A related-family STEP file is useful for exploration but does not qualify the chosen jack or EARS dress nut.
