## Why it is useful

This connector was listed in the original control collection and in the related scope prototype. It is retained to distinguish a generic PCB audio jack from the panel-clamped patch-jack requirement.

## Design cautions

Socket access direction, bushing/thread, switch contacts and footprint must be checked independently. A compact side-entry jack cannot replace a front-panel threaded jack simply because both accept a 3.5 mm plug.
