## Why it is useful

The 500 kΩ linear variant was introduced for the two post-sample slew controls. It is useful when a long RC time constant is needed without enlarging the capacitor bank excessively.

## Design cautions

The high-impedance node must remain local and buffered. Wiper leakage, board cleanliness, capacitor leakage and amplifier bias current can dominate the slow endpoint. FAST is not necessarily a hard bypass, and the same shaft does not imply the same electrical role as B103.
