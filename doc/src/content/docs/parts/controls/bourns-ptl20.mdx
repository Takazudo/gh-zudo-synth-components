## Why it is useful

This 10 kΩ green-LED ordering candidate was investigated for showing the active envelope stage in the moving fader stem. The PTL family provides separate LED connections; the stage indication need not follow the pot position.

## Design cautions

The nominal body is 35 × 9 mm for 20 mm travel. The 10/15/19 mm lever options are actuator heights, not shorter slider travel. Green identity must not be presented as amber. Confirm LED polarity/current, all terminals, slot sweep, wash restrictions and factory soldering before adoption.

## What the project taught us

Research candidate; replaced for this instrument to reduce footprint, sourcing and assembly effort.
