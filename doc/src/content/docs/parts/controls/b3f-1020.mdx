## Why it is useful

A conventional tactile switch is a plausible inexpensive electrical element for manual trigger/sample actions. It becomes a panel control only after the cap, guided plunger, stroke and PCB mounting plane are designed together.

## Design cautions

The familiar 6 × 6 mm switch body does not establish the fingertip surface or exposed height. Account for pretravel, overtravel, alignment, operating force and a mechanical stop. Debounce or shape the pulse where repeated transitions would retrigger the circuit.
