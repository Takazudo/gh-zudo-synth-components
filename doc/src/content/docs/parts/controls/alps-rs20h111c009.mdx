## Why it is useful

The earlier AR design used this 50 kΩ linear slider as its mechanical reference. A 35 mm body was necessary for only 20 mm of travel; that discrepancy is the reusable layout lesson.

## Design cautions

The 4 mm stem dimension along motion adds to the swept opening: 20 mm travel is not a 20 mm panel slot. Add tolerance, cap sweep and working clearance. The retained STEP belongs to the non-illuminated ALPS slider and must never stand in for a Bourns PTL illuminated slider.

## What the project taught us

Retained alternative after R12 replaced all faders with pots and separate stage LEDs.

The originating instrument’s acceptance/rejection is contextual. It does not define a universal recommendation for another synth.
