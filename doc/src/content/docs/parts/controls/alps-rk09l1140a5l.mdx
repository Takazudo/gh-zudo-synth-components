## Why it is useful

This was the original substantial rotary-control candidate. The retained manufacturer bundle includes a STEP body, companion drawing, family PDF and mounting-hardware figures. It remains useful for instruments that prioritize a conventional capped control and panel clamping.

## Design cautions

Its 15 mm shaft-length code is not the exposed height above a panel. The larger nut/washer can dominate spacing even with a small cap. The retained family rating distinguishes a 10 V DC limit from a higher AC rating; a ±12 V end-to-end connection is not acceptable.

## What the project taught us

Early baseline; later removed from the compact instrument, not rejected for all synth use.

The originating instrument’s acceptance/rejection is contextual. It does not define a universal recommendation for another synth.
