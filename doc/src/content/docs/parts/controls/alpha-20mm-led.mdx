## Why it is useful

This family was considered during the SE1 compact-slider investigation. It is retained as a possible source of an LED-in-stem control for other instruments.

## Design cautions

The family does not establish the exact fader inside SE1. Centre-detent, no-detent, resistance, LED color, body length and cap all need exact selection. A 20 mm travel listing alone does not prove a smaller body than PTL20.
