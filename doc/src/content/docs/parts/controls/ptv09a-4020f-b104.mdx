## Why it is useful

This 100 kΩ linear variant shares the mechanical family with B103, allowing a designer to change electrical loading without redrawing the visible actuator. That does not make the two values electrically interchangeable.

## Design cautions

Check the receiving amplifier’s input bias current, leakage and cable pickup. As a divider the maximum Thevenin resistance is near one quarter of the end-to-end value; as a rheostat the whole selected resistance can matter. Recheck every DC-voltage and end-resistance condition.
