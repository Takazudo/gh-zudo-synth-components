## Why it is useful

The 10 kΩ linear member of the bushingless PTV09A-4020F family is a useful starting point for buffered control-voltage dividers. The project moved to the exposed insulated shaft rather than treating a small knob cap as a smaller potentiometer body.

## Design cautions

Choose resistance from loading, noise and current, not from the panel drawing. The family’s 20 V DC limit does not permit connecting it directly between −12 V and +12 V. A bushingless pot needs a real board/support arrangement; its 6 mm shaft is not a 6 mm footprint.
