## Why it is useful

The slide-switch candidate exposed a key preview error: the prototype’s authored rectangle had not been tied to an actual part or cutout.

## Design cautions

Body size, visible tab, travel and PCB-to-panel reach are four separate dimensions. This part does not provide the three maintained states needed by MODE/SYNC. The user preferred toggle ergonomics for the instrument.
