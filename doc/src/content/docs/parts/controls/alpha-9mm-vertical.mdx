## Why it is useful

A familiar synth-shop family was retained alongside JLC/LCSC candidates because avoiding home soldering does not require avoiding ordinary synth controls.

## Design cautions

The shop family label is not an exact manufacturer suffix. Shaft length, detent, taper, resistance, mounting height and PCB pin orientation must all be resolved. Do not copy a Bourns or ALPS footprint on the basis of a 9 mm family description.
