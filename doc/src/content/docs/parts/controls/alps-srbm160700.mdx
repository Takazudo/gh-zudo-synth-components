## Why it is useful

This six-position alternative was considered when the octave selector’s footprint dominated the oscillator pitch. Its operating orientation would require an appropriately oriented interface board.

## Design cautions

Do not compare only body widths. The shaft axis, terminal sweep, supports, connector access and panel bushing still need a full stack. It was not chosen as a drop-in replacement for SRBV160803.
